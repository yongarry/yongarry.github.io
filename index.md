## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Integrated M.S./Ph.D. Program at the [Department of Convergence Science and Technology](https://convergence.snu.ac.kr/), Seoul National University, where I am a graduate researcher at the [DYROS Lab](https://dyros.snu.ac.kr/) (Dynamic Robotics & Systems Lab), formerly supervised by Prof. Jaeheung Park.

My research focuses on making humanoid robots walk reliably in the real world. I work on reinforcement learning-based locomotion policies that respect physical constraints such as footstep placement and torque limits, and on closing the sim-to-real gap so that these policies transfer to hardware. I have also built teleoperation systems for humanoid avatar robots, including work with Team SNU in the ANA Avatar XPRIZE Finals.

## Research Interests

- **Reinforcement Learning:** locomotion policies, footstep constrained policies
- **Sim-to-Real Transfer:** domain randomization, torque-space perturbation, policy smoothness regularization
- **Humanoid Robotics:** bipedal walking, whole-body control, teleoperation and avatar systems
